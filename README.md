A one-image summary of the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn as a newspaper front page: the Trump–Xi–Putin meeting in Shenzhen as the lead story, with the US–Iran Gulf exchange, Anthropic's record IPO, Nvidia's leadership change, the Jeffries Speaker nomination, Voyager 1's light-day milestone and Cyclone Akvan in the grid, plus six briefs. The image is rendered by `render.py` (Python + PIL) into `entry.png` (1024×1024); `main.py` copies those bytes to the output location, so every run writes the identical file.

Honesty note: this entry was built on 19 November 2026 and covers news reported up to the time it was built (about 02:20 UTC, 19 November). Every story was checked against wire and broadsheet reporting of 18–19 November 2026 (AP, Reuters, AFP, BBC, The New York Times, Le Monde, CNN, NPR) before it was drawn; anything that could not be confirmed was left out.

## Run it
